翻译处理原则Translation Principles

  • 菜单路径保留英文原形，后面加中文括注 —— 因为那是你在 Archicad 里真正要点的字 Menu paths keep their original English form with a Chinese note, because that is the text you actually click in Archicad
  • 计算表达式、属性名、枚举值等纯技术标识符保留英文，只译说明文字 —— 改了就对不上官方 Expressions, property names and enum values stay in English; only the explanations are translated — changing them would break parity with the official docs
  • 每篇文章下方可展开「英文原文对照」，随时核对官方原文与原图 Every article has an expandable "Official English Original" section for side-by-side verification
  • 统一术语：库管理器、工作环境、工具箱、配方、工程量清单、属性、分类、特征标记 Consistent terminology: Library Manager, Work Environment, Toolbox, Recipe, Bill of Quantities, Property, Classification, Trait

已知局限Known Limitations

  • 1208 张截图中有 33 张在官方源站已失效（多为老博客关站导致 404），页面以占位说明替代，不显示破图 33 of the screenshots are no longer available on the official server (the old blog was taken down); the pages show a placeholder instead of broken images
  • Electrical 库含欧盟、印度、撒哈拉以南非洲、英国、美国，无中国国标库 The Electrical library covers EU, India, sub-Saharan Africa, UK and US — no Chinese national standard library
  • Sites 为澳新专属，Bracing 为新西兰专属 Sites is for Australia/NZ, Bracing is New Zealand only
  • Archicad 30 起工具包改名 ProKit，菜单名随之改变；Archicad 29 起库包必须手动加载 From Archicad 30 the toolkit is renamed ProKit with different menu names; from Archicad 29 library packs must be loaded manually
